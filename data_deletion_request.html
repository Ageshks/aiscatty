Data Deletion Request

Last updated: August 2026

Your Privacy Matters to Us

At Aiscatty, we respect your right to control your personal data. If you wish to delete your account and all associated data from our platform, you can submit a data deletion request. We will process your request promptly and permanently remove your data from our systems.

What Data Gets Deleted

When you submit a data deletion request, we will permanently remove:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, and profile picture.
  • Pet Listings: All pets you have listed, including photos, videos, and descriptions.
  • Chat Messages: All messages you have exchanged with other users.
  • Favorites & Saved Items: Your saved pets and favorite listings.
  • Adoption Requests: All adoption requests you have sent or received.
  • Location & Device Data: Stored location history and device information.

How to Request Data Deletion

Follow these simple steps to request deletion of your data:

  1. Open the Aiscatty app on your device.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings.
  3. Tap on "Delete My Account" or "Request Data Deletion".
  4. Confirm your choice when prompted.

Alternatively, email us your request at:

support@aiscatty.app

When emailing, please include the email address and phone number registered with your Aiscatty account so we can verify your identity and process the request securely.

What Happens Next

  • Acknowledgment: We will confirm receipt of your request within 3 business days.
  • Processing: Your data will be permanently deleted within 30 days of your request.
  • Confirmation: You will receive an email confirmation once your data has been fully deleted.
Note: After your account is deleted, you will no longer be able to log in to Aiscatty or access any of your listings, chats, or saved data. This action is irreversible.

Need Help?

If you have any questions about the data deletion process or need assistance, please contact us at support@aiscatty.app.
